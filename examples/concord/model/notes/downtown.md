# Downtown Landscape Maintenance District

Each parcel pays the sum of four zone charges:

* **Todos Santos Plaza**: $0.066582 per sq ft of nonresidential parcel area, or
  $20.227564 per home for residential parcels.
* **Streetscape**: $2.019385 per foot of frontage.
* **Collector Street Median**: $0.573620 per qualifying frontage foot plus
  $0.003344 per sq ft.
* **Thoroughfare Median** (every Downtown parcel): $1.418458 per qualifying
  frontage foot plus $0.003371 per sq ft.

Total FY 2026-27 assessments are **$519,152.18** over 767 parcels. Frontage is
not in public parcel data, so the calculator defaults it to 0; compare with
the roll amount shown for each parcel.
