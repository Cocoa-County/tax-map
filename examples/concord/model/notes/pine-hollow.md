# Pine Hollow Estates

Costs are shared by the 401 single-family parcels in proportion to each home's
**original** floor area (Part D, Table 2). Of 425 parcels, 24 open-space or
utility parcels are not assessed.

* Total FY 2026-27 assessment: **$61,755.46**
* Total original floor area: **731,026 sq ft**, about **$0.0845 per sq ft**

The map uses the County's current living area, so homes with additions show a
slightly higher estimate than the roll. The roll amount is what is billed.
