# Citywide Street Lighting Assessment District

Every parcel in the city is assigned equivalent dwelling units (EDU) from its
County land-use code, then charged **$25.00 per EDU**. A single-family home is
1.00 EDU ($25); a shopping center is 20 EDU ($500).

EDU = lighting intensity + pedestrian activity + security factors from
Appendix A. Total FY 2026-27 assessment: **$1,046,853.92**.

No parcel roll is published for this district, so amounts on the map are
computed from the schedule.
