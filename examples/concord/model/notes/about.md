# About this map

This map shows the City of Concord's FY 2026-27 landscape, lighting and street
lighting assessment districts. Districts, zones, rates and parcel amounts come
from the Final Engineer's Reports adopted on May 26, 2026 (Resolutions 26-31
to 26-34). Every number links to the page it came from.

It was built with [taxmap](https://github.com/Cocoa-County/tax-map) from the
research in concord-lnl.
