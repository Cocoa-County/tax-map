Start with a public question

The answer should not require a PDF scavenger hunt.

“What am I paying for?”

“Which district includes my property?”

“How was this assessment calculated?”

The evidence may already be in reports, resolutions, assessment rolls, and parcel data. taxmap gives an AI agent a repeatable workflow for connecting those records—with citations and checks, not just plausible answers.

A new-city walkthrough

Imagine starting in Martinez.

Choose one documented assessment district or public question. Build a useful explanation, then grow as the evidence supports it.

  1. 01

    Bring the records together

    Create a dedicated city project. Add official reports, adopted resolutions, assessment rolls, and available parcel GIS. Keep notes and pictures as context, distinct from authoritative evidence.

  2. 02

    Give your agent a mission

    taxmap supplies agent skills and an MCP server for working with documents, GIS, formulas, and checks. The agent researches and interprets; the tools record its work in reviewable files.

  3. 03

    Review the evidence and the gaps

    Check roll totals and parcel joins. Compare calculations with official amounts. Keep missing inputs, uncertain sources, and mismatches visible instead of hiding them.

  4. 04

    Publish something neighbors can use

    Preview a searchable map with district explanations, sourced calculators, and verified county bill links where available. Approve the public fields, then publish a static site.

Trust is part of the product

AI helps do the work.
It does not become the authority.

Your project stays yours

Sources, rolls, formulas, GIS, and explanations live in ordinary files you can inspect, version, and maintain—not only in a conversation.

Your community decides what is public

Review sources, publication rights, and privacy before deploying. Publish approved fields, not private correspondence or owner information by default.

Your app is portable

Rebuild and publish the static site on GitHub Pages or another web host. Research and building use the Python tools and an MCP-capable agent.

Rolls and county bills are authoritative. Derived outlines are approximate, not legal boundaries. AI-assisted research still requires human review.
